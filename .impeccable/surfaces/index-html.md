# Website surface (index.html, built from site/template.html + site/styles.css)

Scope: the whole product in one self-contained page. The landing story and the full working dashboard are joined.
Visitor mode: **Persuade on top, Operate from the dashboard down.** The audience is hackathon judges on a laptop, projector or phone; farmers are the depicted persona.
Pinned by the user: match the attached NEOFARM reference exactly in style. That means vibrant lime and deep moss on white/light gray, pill shapes, big bold headers with muted body, an aerial-farmland hero with a green overlay and a dual pill group ("Partnership" and "Meet Farmers"), a moss stat band, grid sections for services, solutions and testimonials, and fade/slide motion plus hover lifts.
Truth constraints: the stats are real project numbers, and the testimonials are labelled as illustrative. No fake NASA affiliation.
Later request from the user: make the farm model prettier and more modern.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A premium agritech landing page whose product is live on the page. The reference's marketing grammar (hero, stat band, statement, carousel, dark forecast section, stories, photo CTA) carries the actual working dashboard. This refuses a landing page that only describes a product shown elsewhere.

OWN-WORLD: Lime #b8e55a for primary pills and highlights; moss #0b2e1b / #0f3d24 / #155030 for the hero overlay, stat band, forecast section and the dark "digital farm" map; ground #f3f5f1 with white cards; ink #10231a; muted #5a6b61. Water blue appears only for water. Shapes are rounded-full pills and 24–32px cards. Type is Be Vietnam Pro 800 for display. Photography is verified Unsplash aerial paddies and fields on moss fallbacks.

STORY: A judge reads the promise (NASA data → plain irrigation advice), sees the true numbers, then uses the dashboard. They tap a plot, open "Gợi ý tưới tiêu" and see the water run into plot B, try a what-if in the forecast section, and see the savings, the illustrative stories and the partner CTA.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed aerial paddy photo under a moss gradient. The white 3-line headline sits at the left, with the lime "Hợp tác" and outline "Gặp nông dân" pills. Below them is a glass chip with today's advice (it opens the advice). There is a round scroll arrow at the right and a transparent nav that turns solid on scroll.

FORM: User-pinned reference (NEOFARM). No concept roll, because the brief pins the world. Seed key: none (brief-pinned).

Signature interaction: the farm map is a dark "digital farm". Glossy plots cast soft shadows; data chips float on each plot; the selected plot's lime ring breathes. "Gợi ý tưới tiêu" sends a glowing water line from the gate into the plot, with bright dashes running along it. Rain streaks fall when rain is due.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
